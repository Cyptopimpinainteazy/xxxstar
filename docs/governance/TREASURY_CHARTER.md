# X3 Treasury Charter

## Purpose
The X3 Treasury manages protocol-level funds to support the growth, security, and maintenance of the X3 network.

## Treasury Initial Allocation
| Category | Allocation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Operational Reserve | 5% of genesis supply | Infrastructure, monitoring, validator support |
| Development Fund | 10% of genesis supply | Core protocol development, audits, security |
| Ecosystem Grants | 10% of genesis supply | Developer grants, integrations, community tools |
| Liquidity Provision | 5% of genesis supply | DEX bootstrapping, LP incentives |
| Reserve Buffer | 10% of genesis supply | Emergency response, unplanned costs |

## Spending Thresholds
| Amount | Approval | Notice |
|---|---|---|
| < $10,000 equivalent | Treasury multisig (3-of-5) | None required |
| $10,000 - $100,000 | Treasury multisig + Council info | 7-day public notice |
| > $100,000 | Council proposal + public vote | 14-day public notice |

## Disbursement Process
1. Recipient submits on-chain proposal with rationale
2. Treasury multisig reviews within 7 days
3. Approved disbursements are executed on-chain
4. All transactions are publicly visible on the block explorer

## Prohibited Uses
- Personal compensation to council members
- Market manipulation
- Activities prohibited by applicable law
