# C26 — Web3 Hackathons + Prize Pools

**Purpose:** Catalog every major recurring Web3/crypto hackathon with a verifiable prize pool, sponsor list, and URL. Used by X3 Atomic Star to (a) scout sponsorship/leads, (b) participate as a builder, (c) find talent.
**Source format:** Same column structure as `GRANTS_DATABASE.md` (#, Program, Covers, Award, Eligibility, URL, Fit notes, Status, Confidence).
**Last updated:** 2026-09-05 (initial C26 research batch 1 — ETHGlobal flagship events).
**Verification rule:** URLs checked where feasible; entries not verifiable at write time are marked RESEARCH with confidence 1-2. No fabricated program names or prize pools. No fabrication of sponsor names.

> **Quality note:** Prize pools listed are public, confirmed-from-past-event sources (post-event reports, official prize pages, sponsor announcements). Last 2-3 instances listed where verifiable. Web3 hackathon prizes are typically denominated in USDC/USDT on chain; some events use native tokens (ETH, SOL, MATIC). Winners are paid post-judging. Builders should budget for travel stipends if attending in person.
