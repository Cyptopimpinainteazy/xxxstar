# C25 — VC open intake / scout programs + global accelerators

**Purpose:** Catalog every reputable crypto/Web3 VC with rolling open application or scout program, every reputable accelerator that accepts Web3/crypto infrastructure projects, and every non-dilutive foundation/DAO grant program relevant to a Substrate-based cross-VM atomic-execution project.
**Source format:** Same column structure as `GRANTS_DATABASE.md` (#, Program, Covers, Award, Eligibility, URL, Fit notes, Status, Confidence).
**Last updated:** 2026-09-05 (initial C25 research — VCs, accelerators, foundation grants).
**Verification rule:** URLs checked where feasible; entries not verifiable at write time are marked RESEARCH with confidence 1-2. No fabricated program names.

> **Quality note:** All programs below are real, publicly known programs at time of write. URLs were spot-verified via web_fetch when feasible. Several VCs intentionally do not publish open application forms (closed, referral-only, or partner-NDA-only) and are listed with their public portfolio/contact pages and a low confidence rating. The "Award" column is omitted for pure venture checks where the check size is negotiated per deal; the column is shown as "Check size" instead.
